# Welcome!

I am Amber X. Chen, a PhD candidate in Social Psychology in the [Yu Emotion Science Lab (YES Lab)](https://yeslab.psych.ucsb.edu/) and Master's student in Computer Science at UCSB . My research integrates computational methods (Natural Language Processing, Machine Learning, Social Network Analysis) with traditional social psychology to understand social cognition.

I specialize in building predictive models and analyzing large-scale data using advanced machine learning techniques. My work spans multimodal AI applications, time-series analysis of cultural sentiment, and information spread on social media platforms, often working with datasets containing millions of observations.
