### Couples' Behaviors Study — Get Paid to Participate

Dive into our study exploring how couples coordinate their division of house chores, the emotions involved, and how this shapes individual and relationship well-being.

<p><strong>Compensation:</strong> Each person will receive <strong>$50</strong> and will be entered in a raffle to win <strong>$200</strong>.</p>

<p><strong>Eligibility:</strong> You and your partner should be cohabitating, in a relationship for at least six months, both willing to participate, and 18 years or older.</p>

<p><strong>Format:</strong> The study has two phases:<br/>
1) an intake phase with questionnaires and a Zoom conversation task; <br/>
2) a mobile phase with brief (~3 minutes) morning and evening surveys for two weeks.</p>
